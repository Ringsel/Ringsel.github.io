EmberHeart privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026

In short: EmberHeart has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or any other personal details. Your progress stays on your phone. The Android app can show reward ads from Google AdMob, but only when you choose to watch one, and Google collects some information about your device to show those ads. This page explains what, and how to control it.

Who we are

EmberHeart is made by Vairocana Studios ("we", "us"). For any question about this policy or your information, write to b6825018@gmail.com.

What the game keeps on your phone

EmberHeart saves your progress and settings (stages cleared, gold, gear, sound and display choices) in the app's own storage on your phone. We do not receive this information, and there are no accounts or cloud saves.

  • If your phone's Android backup is on, Android may copy this storage to your Google account so it can move to a new phone. That is Android's own backup, controlled in your phone's settings.
  • Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes your progress.

Reward ads from Google AdMob

The Android app offers optional reward ads: watch an ad and get a reward, such as Energy or a revive. An ad plays only after you tap to watch one. The ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app collects:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • how you use the app and its ads, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data;
  • device identifiers, such as your Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This information goes to Google, not to us, and is encrypted while it is sent. Google describes it in its Google Play data disclosure for AdMob, and explains how it uses it in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before any ad is loaded. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalized ads, in your phone's settings.
  • No ads at all. You never have to watch an ad. Every reward an ad gives can also be had for free once or twice a day, or for embers earned in the game.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own, and sends us nothing.
  • There are no accounts, no chat and no purchases with real money.
  • We do not sell your information.

Children

EmberHeart is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website itself uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, for example by adding a new service, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the game.

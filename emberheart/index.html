The story

The Last Ember

The Crownless King put out every fire in the land. One ember is left, and it is yours. Climb the Ember Road from brazier to brazier, light the fires again and relight every keep he left cold.

The game

Easy to pick up, hard to put down

  • 100 stages, 10 regionsEach road climbs to a lord's keep, and every stage ends with a boss.
  • One thumb is enoughDrag to move and tap Dash to dodge. Your weapons attack on their own.
  • Bosses you can readEvery boss has three moves. Watch the red ground, step out, and strike while he is open.
  • Unite your weaponsAny two skills at their top rank become a new weapon: the Sword and Meteor Foundry make the Meteor Sword.
  • Twenty kinds of enemiesEach one warns you before it strikes and leaves an opening after.
  • Fair to playFree, with ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward.
